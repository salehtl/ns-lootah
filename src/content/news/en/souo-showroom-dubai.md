N S Lootah Motors L.L.C has officially opened the SOUO showroom in Dubai, the brand’s first international showroom outside China. The opening marks a major step in the global expansion of SOUO, the premium motorcycle brand of Great Wall Motor (GWM).

## Distinguished guests at the official opening

The opening ceremony showcased the flagship SOUO S2000 and its 2,000 cc horizontally opposed Flat-8, the world’s first production motorcycle engine of its kind, reflecting SOUO’s approach of combining distinctive design with advanced engineering and technology. The event brought together business leaders, members of the media, influencers, motorcycle enthusiasts and a senior SOUO team from China.

The SOUO delegation was led by Victor Zhao, CEO, and included Miles Liu, Overseas Director; Benny Zhou, Director of IBD; Uwe Moser, Chief Technical Engineer; and Rosie Chen, GCC Regional Manager.

> Through riding events, brand experience days and exclusive owner clubs, SOUO will cultivate its brand culture in target markets and foster a loyal community of enthusiasts. Leveraging Dubai’s position as a regional hub, SOUO will systematically expand into Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman, building a comprehensive footprint in the Middle East’s premium motorcycle market.
>
> — Victor Zhao, CEO, GWM SOUO

> Opening the first SOUO showroom outside China here in Dubai marks the beginning of a long-term partnership and a shared ambition to build a strong presence for the brand in the UAE and the wider region. Choosing Dubai for this milestone reflects the city’s global position as a market that embraces innovation, technology and exceptional mobility experiences.
>
> — Abdulla Alaraidi, General Manager, N S Lootah Motors L.L.C

## SOUO S2000 CL: a new definition of the luxury cruiser

The SOUO S2000 CL sets a new standard for luxury cruisers with its 1,999 cc naturally aspirated, horizontally opposed eight-cylinder engine, delivering 153.68 horsepower and 190 Nm of torque. It is matched with an 8-speed wet dual-clutch transmission (DCT), taking the motorcycle from 0 to 100 km/h in about 3.9 seconds to an electronically limited top speed of 200 km/h, while a shaft final drive keeps power delivery smooth and steady.

The S2000 CL is built on a high-strength die-cast aluminium frame. It weighs 427 kg, with a seat height of 690 mm and a wheelbase of 1,810 mm, and features Hossack-style front suspension with electronically adjustable damping and a Brembo braking system with twin 320 mm front discs, a 295 mm rear disc and four-piston callipers.

Its technology includes a 4-inch circular TFT display, the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8155 platform, navigation and music connectivity, and Travel, Rain and Sport riding modes, along with traction control, cruise control and tyre-pressure monitoring. Premium features include heated seats and grips, intelligent LED lighting, a reverse function and low-speed parking assistance.

By combining a 2.0-litre Flat-8 engine, an 8-speed DCT, advanced suspension, smart technology and top-tier comfort, the S2000 CL brings a distinctive, refined experience to the luxury motorcycle market.

The opening reflects the UAE’s growing importance as a hub for global brands and advanced mobility. Through this partnership, N S Lootah Motors aims to build a lasting presence for SOUO in the UAE, develop a strong community around the brand and lay the foundation for future growth across the region.

## About N S Lootah Motors

N S Lootah Motors L.L.C is the automotive company of N S Lootah Group and the official distributor of SOUO Motorcycles in the United Arab Emirates. In late 2025, Great Wall Soul Technology Co. Ltd., part of China’s Great Wall Motor (GWM), named N S Lootah Motors its exclusive distributor in the UAE.
